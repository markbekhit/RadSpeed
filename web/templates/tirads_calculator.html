How ACR TI-RADS 2017 works

TI-RADS adds points across five ultrasound features. The total decides the risk level and whether fine-needle aspiration (FNA) or follow-up ultrasound is advised, based on the nodule's largest diameter.

LevelPointsEst. malignancyFNAFollow-up US
TR100.3%NoneNone
TR21–21.5%NoneNone
TR334.8%≥2.5 cm≥1.5 cm (1, 3, 5 yr)
TR44–69.1%≥1.5 cm≥1.0 cm (1, 2, 3, 5 yr)
TR5≥7≥35%≥1.0 cm≥0.5 cm (annually to 5 yr)

Source: Tessler FN et al., ACR Thyroid Imaging, Reporting and Data System (TI-RADS): White Paper of the ACR TI-RADS Committee, J Am Coll Radiol 2017.
