How the radiology impression generator works

Use it for the narrow drafting step between completed findings and your final impression.

1. Paste de-identified findings

Add the completed findings and optional study name. Do not include names, record numbers or other patient identifiers.

2. Generate a draft

Choose whether to include guideline-aware recommendations. Your browser-saved style settings control wording and format.

3. Review, edit and copy

Check every statement, recommendation and measurement before you move the draft into the clinical report.

Current limit: RadSpeed works only from the text you provide. It does not interpret images or replace radiologist review.
