Current limits

Know what the companion does before you install it.

It drafts, not diagnoses

RadSpeed formats the selected text. It does not read the images or replace radiologist review.

It needs network access

The workstation must reach radspeed.com.au unless your practice runs a configured self-hosted service.

Jump keys vary by site

PowerScribe templates do not share one universal impression shortcut. You can set the keys your site already uses.

The publisher warning remains

Windows may show an “Unknown publisher” warning until commercial Authenticode signing is complete. RadSpeed update files have separate integrity signatures.
