### Exam:
CT Chest, Abdomen and Pelvis — Staging

### Technique:
Contrast-enhanced CT of the chest, abdomen and pelvis in the portal venous phase.

### Clinical Details
**Instructions:**
- Record clinical details exactly as dictated. Do not rephrase the wording.
- Exclude patient identifiers, age and gender from the report.
- If not provided: "No clinical details provided."

### Comparison:
**Instructions:**
- List the prior study type and date/year exactly as dictated.
- Put interval change in Findings, not in this section.
- If not provided: "No prior imaging available for comparison."

### Findings:
**Instructions:**
- Use the bold component headings below in this exact order.
- Preserve every dictated finding and measurement under the correct component.
- For staging studies, give the site, size and interval change of the primary tumour, target lesions, suspicious nodes and metastases when dictated.
- Do not invent a cancer stage, treatment response, target lesion or interval change.
- Combine normal structures within one component. Use precise normal wording for structures not dictated.

**Lungs and Airways**
- Include pulmonary nodules or masses by lobe, size and interval change when stated.
- Default: "Lungs clear. No suspicious pulmonary nodule or mass. Airways patent."

**Pleura**
- Default: "No pleural effusion, pneumothorax or pleural nodularity."

**Mediastinum, Hila and Axillae**
- Describe suspicious lymph nodes by station and short-axis size when stated.
- Default: "No mediastinal, hilar or axillary lymphadenopathy."

**Heart and Great Vessels**
- Default: "Heart normal in size. No pericardial effusion. Thoracic aorta normal in calibre."

**Liver and Biliary System**
- Describe focal lesions by segment, size and interval change when stated.
- Default: "Liver unremarkable with no focal lesion. Gallbladder and biliary system unremarkable."

**Spleen and Pancreas**
- Default: "Spleen and pancreas unremarkable."

**Adrenal Glands, Kidneys and Ureters**
- Default: "Adrenal glands and kidneys unremarkable. No hydronephrosis."

**Bowel, Mesentery and Peritoneum**
- Include peritoneal or omental disease and ascites when present.
- Default: "Bowel, mesentery and peritoneum unremarkable. No ascites or peritoneal nodularity."

**Pelvic Organs and Urinary Bladder**
- Default: "Pelvic organs and urinary bladder unremarkable."

**Lymph Nodes**
- Describe suspicious abdominal and pelvic nodes by chain and short-axis size when stated.
- Default: "No abdominal or pelvic lymphadenopathy."

**Vasculature**
- Default: "Abdominal aorta and major abdominal vessels unremarkable."

**Bones and Soft Tissues**
- Preserve dictated osseous and soft-tissue findings exactly.
- Default: "No aggressive osseous lesion. No significant chest wall or abdominopelvic soft-tissue abnormality."

[correct spellings] CT chest abdomen pelvis staging restaging primary tumour target lesion pulmonary metastasis hepatic metastasis lymphadenopathy mediastinal hilar axillary para-aortic retroperitoneal iliac peritoneal omental osseous RECIST [correct spellings]

### Impression:
**Instructions:**
- Give a concise staging summary in numbered points.
- Lead with the primary tumour and its interval change when known.
- Then summarise regional nodes, distant metastases and other clinically important findings.
- State treatment response only when supported by a suitable comparison and dictated findings.
- Do not include minor incidental findings unless clinically relevant.
