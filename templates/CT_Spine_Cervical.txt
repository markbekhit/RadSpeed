### Exam:
CT Cervical Spine

### Technique:
Axial cuts were obtained from the skull base to the cervicothoracic junction. Sagittal and coronal reformatting were then obtained. Bone and soft tissue window reconstructions were reviewed.

### Clinical Details
**Instructions:**
- Record clinical details exactly as dictated. Do not rephrase or reformulate the wording.
- **Exclude** patient name, date of birth, age, gender, and MRN/accession numbers from the report, even if mentioned in the transcript.
- **If not provided:** 'No clinical details provided.'

### Comparison:
**Instructions:**
- **If provided:** List the prior imaging studies mentioned (modality and date/year if stated). Do not summarise changes here — comparison findings belong in the Findings section.
- **If not provided:** 'No prior imaging available for comparison.'

### Findings:
**Instructions:**
- Use this exact internal order within Findings: **overview first, then levels, then final checks**. Do not create extra structure-by-structure subsections.
- **Overview:** Begin with one concise unlabelled paragraph covering cervical alignment and vertebral body heights, including any fracture or destructive osseous lesion, followed by the craniocervical junction/odontoid. Preserve dictated wording when supplied; otherwise use compact normal wording.
- **Levels:** Then report C2/3, C3/4, C4/5, C5/6, C6/7 and C7/T1 in anatomical order, regardless of dictation order. Give every level its own bold heading, e.g. **C5/6:**. Gather disc-space/osteophyte, endplate, uncovertebral and facet change, canal/foraminal stenosis and neural compromise at the relevant level rather than repeating these later.
- Keep normal level descriptions brief, e.g. **C2/3:** No significant disc osteophyte complex, spinal canal or foraminal stenosis.
- **Final checks:** After C7/T1, add only a concise survey of the prevertebral soft tissues and paravertebral musculature, in that order. Include posterior fossa, cervicothoracic or other extraspinal findings only when explicitly dictated; do not add routine normal headings for them.
- Do not generate separate headings or repeated summaries for vertebral bodies, posterior elements, endplates, uncovertebral joints, facet joints, spinal canal or neural foramina. These belong in the overview or at the relevant level.
- **Commonly Misspelled Words:**
[correct spellings] osteophyte, spondylosis, spondylolisthesis, foraminal, myelopathy, radiculopathy, uncovertebral, facet, listhesis, odontoid, atlantoaxial, prevertebral [correct spellings]
- If 'query' is mentioned in the transcript, it should be rendered as '?' in the report.

### Impression:
**Instructions:**
- Create an impression from findings, into a brief description in bullet points.
- Include only clinically relevant findings. Minor incidental findings that are fully described in the body do not need to be restated here.
- For a normal/negative study, include a closing statement where appropriate, e.g. 'No cause for the patient's symptoms identified on this examination.'
- **Sample:**
  - Cervical spondylosis with [level-specific] disc osteophyte complex causing [mild/moderate/severe] foraminal narrowing.
  - *No acute fracture or subluxation identified.*
