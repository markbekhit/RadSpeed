### Exam:
CT Thoracic Spine

### Technique:
Non-contrast CT of the thoracic spine.

### Clinical Details
**Instructions:**
- Record clinical details exactly as dictated. Do not rephrase or reformulate the wording.
- **Exclude** patient name, date of birth, age, gender, and MRN/accession numbers from the report, even if mentioned in the transcript.
- **If not provided:** 'No clinical details provided.'

### Comparison:
**Instructions:**
- **If provided:** List the prior imaging studies mentioned (modality and date/year if stated). Do not summarise changes here — comparison findings belong in the Findings section.
- **If not provided:** 'No prior imaging available for comparison.'

### Findings:
**Instructions:**
- Use this exact internal order within Findings: **overview first, then levels, then final checks**. Do not create extra structure-by-structure subsections.
- **Overview:** Begin with one concise unlabelled paragraph covering thoracic alignment/kyphosis and vertebral body heights, including any fracture or destructive osseous lesion. Preserve dictated wording when supplied; otherwise use compact normal wording.
- **Levels:** Then report each abnormal thoracic level under its own bold heading in anatomical order from T1/2 to T12/L1, regardless of dictation order. Gather disc-space/osteophyte, endplate and facet change, canal/foraminal stenosis and neural compromise at the relevant level. Account for normal levels in one brief **Remaining levels:** line; do not itemise twelve normal levels separately.
- **Final checks:** Finish with one concise survey of the paravertebral soft tissues. Mention costovertebral/costotransverse joints or visualised thoracic organs only when explicitly dictated; do not add routine normal statements for them.
- Do not generate separate headings or repeated summaries for vertebral bodies, posterior elements, facet joints, spinal canal or neural foramina. These belong in the overview or at the relevant level.
- **Commonly Misspelled Words:**
[correct spellings] vertebra, spondylosis, spondylolisthesis, kyphosis, scoliosis, pedicle, paraspinal, costovertebral, osteophyte, compression fracture, Schmorl's node [correct spellings]
- If 'query' is mentioned in the transcript, it should be rendered as '?' in the report.

### Impression:
**Instructions:**
- Create an impression from findings, into a brief description in bullet points.
- Include only clinically relevant findings. Minor incidental findings that are fully described in the body do not need to be restated here.
- Translate moderate foraminal stenosis into potential irritation of the relevant exiting nerve root, and marked/severe foraminal stenosis into likely compression of that root, naming the side and root when the level permits. Do not make this inference for mild, canal or subarticular stenosis, or contradict an explicitly dictated nerve-root relationship.
- For a normal/negative study, include a closing statement where appropriate, e.g. 'No cause for the patient's symptoms identified on this examination.'
- **Sample:**
  - Thoracic spondylosis with [level-specific] findings.
  - *No acute fracture or significant cord compromise identified.*
